      Playing time per player

      Each game uses 4 players, so the expected games per player = open play hours × games per court per hour × courts × 4 ÷ players. With the defaults (3 hours, 4 games per court per hour), 1 court and 12 players gives 4 games per player. Refund = price × (1 − games played ÷ expected games), rounded to the nearest peso and never below ₱0. Only finished games count (a game counts once you press its winner button), so end the games in progress when play is disrupted. Playing time is shown for reference only.

      Pick a waiting player or type a new arrival. “Auto” slots are filled by the queue. A locked game keeps these players, and the same logic continues around it.
